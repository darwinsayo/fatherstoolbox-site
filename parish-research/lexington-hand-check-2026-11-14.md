# Diocese of Lexington hand-check, 14 Nov 2026

Re-check of the 31 Lexington records in `parishcheck/lexington-*.json`, done the same way as the Covington check of 6 Nov 2026. Research only: the map and the hand-check JSON files were not changed.

## Rules followed

- Times come only from the parish's own website, or from the site of the parish that serves it (mother parish), including that parish's own bulletin. No MassTimes or similar sites. Facebook is not a website.
- No time was guessed. Communion services are not Masses.
- Home, Mass times, Sacraments/Reconciliation pages and the latest bulletin were opened where they could be reached.
- The Diocese of Lexington parish finder (cdlex.org/parish-finder/) was used only to see which website a parish lists, and for phone and address.
- Pages that refused automated reading (robots.txt) were not worked around. Pages that returned an error were tried once more the same day.
- All times are Eastern.

- So: Sat 4:30 PM (not on 4th Saturdays), Sun 10:00 AM, Sun 1:00 PM (Congolese), Tue 5:00 PM, Wed 5:00 PM, Thu 12:00 PM (new).

- So: Tue 4:30-4:45 PM, Wed 4:30-4:45 PM, Sun 9:15-9:45 AM, Sat 3:45-4:15 PM (1st-3rd Saturdays), appointments.
- Phone (859) 254-0030 and 485 W. Fourth St. match the record.

### St. Mary, Perryville (mission of St. Andrew, Harrodsburg)
Source: St. Andrew's own site https://www.harrodsburgcatholic.com/ and its bulletins of 27 Sep and 4 Oct 2026 (https://irp.cdn-website.com/df3bd759/files/uploaded/Bulletin+10.04.26.pdf, Bulletin+09.27.26.pdf).

- Bulletin header, quoted: "Saint Mary Catholic Mission 307 South Buell Street, Perryville KY ... Weekend Mass: Saturday Mass - 5:30 PM Holy Day Mass: Mass of the Day 5:00 PM".
