# Archdiocese of Cincinnati hand-check, 14 Nov 2026

Re-check of the 25 Archdiocese of Cincinnati records in `parishcheck/cincinnati-*.json`, done the same way as the Covington check of 6 Nov 2026. Research only: the map and the hand-check JSON files were not changed.

## Rules followed

- Times come only from the parish's own website, or its family-of-parishes site where that site names the church. No MassTimes, Discover Mass or similar sites, and not the Catholic Telegraph. Facebook is not a website.
- No time was guessed. Communion and "worship" services are not counted as Masses unless the site calls them Masses.
- Home, Mass times, Sacraments/Reconciliation pages and the latest bulletin were opened where they could be reached. Bulletin links that go to Discover Mass were not opened.
- Pages that refused automated reading (robots.txt) were not worked around. Pages that returned an error were tried once more the same day.
- All times are Eastern.

## Map corrections

- **St. Simon**: https://stsimonparish.org/ says "Mass Schedule: (changed as of June 5, 2021) Saturday: 7:00P Sunday: 11:00A" and "Monday – 8:00A, St. Simon the Apostle; Tuesday – 8:00A, St. Aloysius on the Ohio; Thursday – 8:00A, St. Aloysius on the Ohio; Friday – 8:00A, St. Simon the Apostle." The map puts the Tue and Thu 8 AM Masses at St. Simon; they belong to St. Aloysius on the Ohio, which already has them on the map (its own site, https://www.stalsontheohio.org/, agrees). St. Simon should keep Mon and Fri 8 AM only. No confession posted. The schedule is still dated 2021.
- **St. Leo the Great**: the home page http://www.saint-leo.org/ lists only "Saturday 6:00 pm Spanish", "Sunday 10:30 am" and "Thursday 7:00 pm". The map's Wednesday 11:30 AM is not there; it may be on the "Worship Schedule" page, which could not be reached. The page reads like a pandemic-era reopening notice. No confession posted. Saturday 6 PM is in Spanish.
- **St. Stephen**: Mass times match (https://riverviewcatholics.org/about-us/mass-times-2/: Sat 6:00 PM, Sun 10:00 AM, Mon-Fri 9:00 AM, all at St. Stephen). But the parish page https://riverviewcatholics.org/saint-stephen/ gives "320 Donhan Ave, Cincinnati, OH 45226" and "Parish Office: 513-871-3373"; the map has 3804 Eastern Ave and no phone. The family's confessions (https://riverviewcatholics.org/sacraments/reconciliation/) are all at other churches.
- **Mother of Christ**: the map links it to ourladyofdivineprovidence.church, but that family is Assumption, St. Bartholomew, St. Bernard, St. Clare and St. Vivian. Mother of Christ is not on it, so the website on record is wrong. Its own site was not found.
