What we do

One tool for your parish’s communication.

Large parishes rarely lack effort. They lack connection between the tools they use. The bulletin lives in one program, the website in another, social media in a third, and sign-ups somewhere else. Staff retype the same message again and again, and parishioners still miss it. Father’s Tool Box replaces that patchwork with one customized tool, built around how your parish actually works.

Better processes

We study how your parish communicates today and remove the steps that slow it down.

One integrated tool

We connect what you already use into a single tool made for your parish, so one update reaches every channel.

Every channel, one source

Website design, print and electronic communication, and social media all draw from the same message.

Support that lasts

We watch how it works in practice and keep improving it.

Through the liturgical year

Ready before each season begins.

A parish lives by the rhythm of the Church’s year. We plan your communication around it, so the schedules and invitations for each season are in people’s hands before it arrives.

  1. Advent

    Violet

    Penance services and Christmas Mass times, announced early.

  2. Christmas

    White & gold

    A warm welcome for the many who come home for Christmas.

  3. Ordinary Time

    Green

    Formation, ministries and the steady life of the parish.

  4. Lent

    Violet

    Ash Wednesday, Stations of the Cross, Confession and fish fries.

  5. Triduum

    White & red

    Holy Thursday, Good Friday and the Easter Vigil, clearly scheduled.

  6. Easter

    White & gold

    Fifty days of rejoicing, First Communions and new Catholics welcomed.

  7. Pentecost

    Red

    Confirmations and the close of the Easter season.

How we work

A simple, steady process.

Assessment, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Each step builds on the one before it, and you are part of every one.

  1. I

    Assessment

    Strategy development

    We assess your parish’s communication needs and the wider landscape, then build a strategy tailored to your community.

    “Which of you wishing to construct a tower does not first sit down and calculate the cost…?”Luke 14:28
  2. II

    Implementation

    Execution & integration

    We put that strategy into action with the right tools to strengthen outreach and engagement across the parish.

    “Be doers of the word and not hearers only…”James 1:22
  3. III

    Monitoring

    Performance tracking

    We keep watch on how each channel is performing and adjust as we go, so good ideas get better.

    “Take good care of your flocks, give careful attention to your herds…”Proverbs 27:23
  4. IV

    Evaluation

    Results assessment

    After each phase we review the results with you and recommend what to carry forward.

    “Test everything; retain what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:21
