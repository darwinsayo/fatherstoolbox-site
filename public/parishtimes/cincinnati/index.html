Hand-curated data

Every time here was read from the parish’s own website.

No third-party Mass-time apps. No scraped directories. We went through the Archdiocese of Cincinnati one parish at a time, read each parish’s own website, and copied down only what it says, as of October 2026. A Facebook page is not counted as a website.

This map covers the archdiocese’s parishes in Hamilton County and the suburbs of Butler, Warren and Clermont counties that lie within about 21 miles of the Cathedral Basilica of St. Peter in Chains, not all 19 counties. Names, addresses and phones come from the archdiocese’s own parish directory; times come only from each parish’s website.

If a parish’s site doesn’t post a time, we leave it blank rather than guess. Blanks are being confirmed by hand, and parishes with no website are being confirmed directly.

Spot a wrong time? Tell us which parish and what it should say, and we will fix it.
