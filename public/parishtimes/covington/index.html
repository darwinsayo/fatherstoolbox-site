Hand-curated data

Every time here was read from the parish’s own website.

No third-party Mass-time apps. No scraped directories. We went through the Diocese of Covington one parish at a time, read each parish’s own website, and copied down only what it says, as of October 2026.

If a parish’s site doesn’t post a time, we leave it blank rather than guess. Blanks are being confirmed by hand, and parishes with no website are being confirmed directly.

Spot a wrong time? Tell us which parish and what it should say, and we will fix it.
