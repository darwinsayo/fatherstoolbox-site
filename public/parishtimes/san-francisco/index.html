Hand-curated data

Every time here was read from the parish’s own website.

No third-party Mass-time apps. No scraped directories. We went through the Archdiocese of San Francisco one parish at a time, read each parish’s own website, and copied down only what it says, as of November 2026. A Facebook page is not counted as a website.

All times are Pacific time, as the parishes post them. The live clock also runs on Pacific time.

If a parish’s site doesn’t post a time, we leave it blank rather than guess. Blanks are being confirmed by hand, and parishes with no website are being confirmed directly.

Spot a wrong time? Tell us which parish and what it should say, and we will fix it.

Mass and confession times at Catholic parishes in the Archdiocese of San Francisco

This map covers the parishes of the Archdiocese of San Francisco, which serves the City and County of San Francisco and Marin and San Mateo counties, from Tomales and Novato in the north through San Rafael and the city itself south along the Peninsula to Daly City, San Mateo, Redwood City, Menlo Park and Half Moon Bay. Switch between Mass and confession, and the map marks the parishes where one is happening now or starting within two hours. Every time comes from the parish’s own website and is shown in Pacific time. Many parishes here celebrate Mass in Spanish, Cantonese, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Latin and other languages, and the language is shown where the parish gives it. Schedules change around holy days and in summer, so call the parish before a trip.
