About

Communication in service of the parish.

Father’s Tool Box exists to give pastors and parish staff the tools to communicate well, so more time can go to ministry.

At Father’s Tool Box, we specialize in website design, communication strategies in both print and electronic form, and social media strategies tailored specifically for Catholic parishes.

We are committed to rethinking parish communication with fresh approaches and modern technology, and to achieving excellent results for your parish’s communication needs.

Whether you are starting from scratch or refreshing what you already have, we work alongside you to build something your parishioners will actually use.

In the Church’s tradition

Not a new idea, but an old calling.

The Church has always used the best means of her day to share the Gospel. Two witnesses shape how we think about our work.

Second Vatican Council, 1963

Inter Mirifica

The Council’s decree on the media of social communication takes its name from its opening words, “among the wonderful” inventions of human skill. It asks pastors and the faithful to put these means to work in service of the Gospel.

It also called for an annual World Communications Day, which most dioceses keep on the Sunday before Pentecost.

Patron of writers · Feast, January 24

St. Francis de Sales

As a young priest in the Chablais, when few would come to hear him preach, he wrote short leaflets explaining the faith and slipped them under doors.

In 1923 Pope Pius XI named him patron of writers and the Catholic press. We think he would have understood the parish bulletin and the parish website very well.

Services

What we offer.

Three services that work best together, each shaped around the life of your parish.

Website design

A clear, welcoming parish website where people can find Mass times, sacraments, ministries and news without hunting for them.

  • Mass, Confession and Adoration times
  • Baptism, First Communion, Confirmation and Marriage preparation
  • OCIA for those becoming Catholic
  • Bulletin archive and online giving

Print & electronic communication

Bulletins, flyers, newsletters and email that carry one consistent message from the pulpit to the pew to the inbox.

  • The weekly bulletin
  • Advent, Lent and Holy Week schedules
  • Parish newsletters and email
  • Flyers for missions, retreats and parish feasts

Social media strategy

A steady, faithful presence online that invites parishioners to engage and helps newcomers find their way to you.

  • Sunday reflections and feast days
  • Parish life, sacraments and celebrations
  • Livestreamed Masses and events
  • A welcome for newcomers and those returning

How we work

Four steps, start to finish.

Assessment, implementation, monitoring and evaluation. Each step builds on the one before it, and you are part of every one.

  1. I

    Assessment

    Strategy development

    We assess your parish’s communication needs and the wider landscape, then build a strategy tailored to your community.

    “Which of you wishing to construct a tower does not first sit down and calculate the cost…?”Luke 14:28
  2. II

    Implementation

    Execution & integration

    We put that strategy into action with the right tools to strengthen outreach and engagement across the parish.

    “Be doers of the word and not hearers only…”James 1:22
  3. III

    Monitoring

    Performance tracking

    We keep watch on how each channel is performing and adjust as we go, so good ideas get better.

    “Take good care of your flocks, give careful attention to your herds…”Proverbs 27:23
  4. IV

    Evaluation

    Results assessment

    After each phase we review the results with you and recommend what to carry forward.

    “Test everything; retain what is good.”1 Thessalonians 5:21
