A small shelf of Catholic classics.

Introduction to the Devout Life St. Francis de Sales

St. Francis de Sales

Introduction to the Devout Life

Written in 1609 for people living in the world, in families, at work and at court, to show that holiness is for every state of life. Five parts: resolving on a devout life, prayer and the sacraments, the virtues, temptations, and renewing the soul.

The Imitation of Christ Thomas à Kempis

Thomas à Kempis

The Imitation of Christ

The best-loved book of devotion after the Bible, written in the early fifteenth century for those who want to follow Christ closely. Four short books: counsels for the spiritual life, the inner life, inward consolation, and the Blessed Sacrament. Here in Bishop Challoner’s translation.

The Baltimore Catechism Third Plenary Council of Baltimore

Third Plenary Council of Baltimore

The Baltimore Catechism

The catechism that taught the faith to generations of American Catholics. This is No. 2, the edition for Confirmation classes: 421 questions and answers in 37 lessons, with prayers and hymns.

Orthodoxy G. K. Chesterton

G. K. Chesterton

Orthodoxy

Chesterton’s account of how he came to believe the Christian faith, written in 1908 before he became a Catholic. Witty, surprising, and still one of the best defences of the Creed.

Douay-Rheims Version Challoner revision

Challoner revision

The Holy Bible, Douay-Rheims Version

The Catholic Bible in English, translated from the Latin Vulgate. The Old Testament was first published at Douay in 1609 and 1610 and the New Testament at Rheims in 1582; Bishop Richard Challoner revised both in 1749 to 1752. Seventy-three books, with Challoner’s notes.
