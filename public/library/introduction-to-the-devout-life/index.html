Introduction to the Devout Life

St. Francis de Sales. Written in 1609 for people living in the world, in families, at work and at court, to show that holiness is for every state of life. Five parts: resolving on a devout life, prayer and the sacraments, the virtues, temptations, and renewing the soul.
